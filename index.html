FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How does the mobile showroom work?

Simply book a free consultation. We bring a curated selection of flooring samples to your home so you can see colors, textures and finishes in your own lighting — no showroom pressure, no driving around town.

How much does new flooring cost?

Costs vary by material and square footage, which is why every estimate is free and transparent. We offer competitive pricing on quality products to fit any budget — you'll know the full price before any work begins.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve all of Los Angeles County and Orange County — including Santa Monica, Altadena, Pasadena, Burbank and surrounding communities.

What types of flooring do you install?

Hardwood, laminate, tile, carpet, stone and more — plus sanding, finishing, custom staining, repair, leveling, waterproofing and staircase installation.

How soon can you start?

We respond to every request within one business hour and offer same-week appointments in most areas. Request your free estimate and we'll confirm availability right away.
